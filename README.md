### Projects

**[Nordic Equity Risk Analyzer](https://github.com/danielengerby1/Nordic-Equity-Analyser)** · [live dashboard](https://nordic-equity-analyser.vercel.app)
Screens 21 Nordic large caps on risk-adjusted return in SEK, builds a max-Sharpe portfolio, and tests it out of sample.
Finding: re-optimising quarterly without hindsight beat OMXS30 over 2018–2026, but a simple equal-weight portfolio did better.
`Python` `pandas` `TypeScript` `Next.js` `walk-forward backtesting`

**[Swedish Rates & Inflation Monitor](https://github.com/danielengerby1/Swedish-rates-monitor)** · [live dashboard](https://swedish-rates-monitor.vercel.app)
The Swedish yield curve, inflation vs the Riksbank's target, mortgage spreads, and bond and household calculators,
built on official Riksbank and SCB data that a scheduled pipeline refreshes and validates every weekday.
`Python` `GitHub Actions` `fixed income` `duration & convexity` `TypeScript`
